# Changelog

## v1.0.1
- Fixed chat messages not reaching other players when the mod was also installed on a dedicated server. The mod now does nothing on a server

## v1.0.0
- World Chat split out of NhxQualityPack, as a replacement for BetterChat: normal chat messages (and `/s` shouts) reach every player in the world, with timestamps, a sound alert for other players' messages, and messages kept while respawning
- Won't load together with NhxQualityPack or BetterChat
